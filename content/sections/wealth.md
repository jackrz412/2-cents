Thinking about wealth concentration is difficult because, very quickly, the values involved move from barely understandable into the abstract. I can picture 10 hot dogs in my head. I cannot picture 1 million hot dogs. I certainly cannot picture 100 billion hot dogs.

We tend to imagine a population where some have very little, others some, some a lot, and a very few a whole, whole lot. That picture is quickly punctured by magnitude (greatness in size). On a number line, values separated by a factor of 10 get very far from each other very fast. Try it below.
