We have an incredibly diverse economy: we lead in finance, technology, and healthcare, and remain a manufacturing heavyweight.

The dollar is the global reserve currency and the medium of exchange in most international trade, which creates unique and unparalleled demand for our money (and for the Treasury bonds above). We maintain one of the highest levels of human capital in the world, with heavy investment in cutting-edge research and science. Many of the world's top universities are American, and many of their graduates, including international students, stay to support the US economy through employment and business ventures.

And American geography is awesome. Our land is so large and diverse that we effectively have a continent-sized free-trade area, where resources and people can move freely.
